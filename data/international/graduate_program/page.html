REQUIREMENTS

General Requirements

  • High school or equivalent school graduates from the year 2022 onward
  • All documents are submitted online on the registration account

Document Requirements

  • Passport
  • Latest color photograph (full face without hat, glasses, or accessories)
  • Master's degree certificate
  • Master's degree transcript
  • English proficiency must be demonstrated by one of the following TOEFL (www.ets.org): TOEFL ITP 500 or TOEFL iBT 61; IELTS (http://ielts.org) with minimum score 5.5; FCE and CAE from Cambridge Main Suite Exams (https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/) with minmimum score 154.
  • Health certificate
  • Statement of Purpose
  • Letters of Recommendation

Notes:

  • All documents are submitted online on the registration account.
  • All courses are delivered in English
  • Please visit the faculty’s website for specific requirements
