Prospective Students

Universitas Indonesia welcomes students from around the world to experience high-quality education in a vibrant and diverse academic community. With campuses in Depok and Central Jakarta, UI offers a dynamic learning environment that combines academic excellence, cultural richness, and global engagement. Prospective international students can choose from a wide range of programs, supported by dedicated services to ensure a smooth academic and cultural transition in Indonesia.

Why study

at Universitas Indonesia

World-Class Education, Global Recognition

Consistently ranked Indonesia’s top university and among the world’s top 200 (QS 2025), UI offers internationally accredited programs in medicine, engineering, business, law, and social sciences, delivering globally respected bachelor’s, master’s, and PhD degrees.

Affordable Excellence, Big Impact

Access elite education at a fraction of the cost. UI tuition ranges from IDR 16.6–64.5 million per semester (USD 1,006–3,909), with 150+ scholarships annually, including full-ride options, making world-class education accessible worldwide.

A Cultural Journey, Every Day

Located in Depok, West Java, UI is a vibrant multicultural community with students from 30+ nationalities. Experience Indonesia’s rich traditions while building the cross-cultural skills essential for today’s interconnected world.

Research that Matters

From sustainable architecture to global health innovation, UI’s research leads solutions to global challenges. Collaborate with top experts in bioengineering, climate change, and policy through strong international partnerships.

An Exciting Life Beyond the Classroom

Enjoy affordable living, a thriving student life, and unparalleled access to Indonesia’s natural beauty and cultural wonders.

Financial Support

UI offers a range of scholarships exclusively for international students, including full and partial funding, ensuring that financial barriers don’t stand in the way of your global academic journey.

FAQ

What is the International Undergraduate Program (IUP/KKI)?

It is a special bachelor’s degree program designed to give students an international quality education while studying in Indonesia. It is also called Kelas Khusus Internasional (KKI).

In IUP, all courses are taught in English, and it provides an opportunity to study abroad. There are 3 schemes, that is Single Degree (SD), Double/Joint Degree. and Fast Track Program.

What makes the KKI Program different from the regular program?

All courses are delivered in English, and students have the option to obtain a single degree from Universitas Indonesiaor a double/joint degree from Universitas Indonesia and the program’s partner university abroad.

What is the difference between a single degree and a double /joint degree program?

The single-degree program means you study for 7 semesters at UI and a required 1-semester (minimum) study abroad for Indonesian students or an optional one-semester study abroad for International students and earn a bachelor’s degree from Universitas Indonesia.

The double/joint-degree program lets you study at UI for about 2 years (depending on partner university policy), then continue at a partner university abroad for one or 2 years. After finishing, you will receive two bachelor’s degrees, one from UI and the other one from the partner university. This program gives you more international exposure and experience living and studying in another country.

Can international students apply for the KKI Program at Universitas Indonesia?

Yes. The program welcomes both Indonesian and non-Indonesian students

What is the admission selection process for the international applicants?

Selection based on desk evaluation (not written test) of academic achievement of the applicant. Desk evaluation made upon the basis of applicant’s profile and document

Only outstanding applicants who meet the requirements will be accepted.

What schemes are available in the KKI Program?

There are three schemes available in the KKI Program:

  1. Single Degree (SD)

A type of KKI Program in which students complete all courses at Universitas Indonesia, with a minimum of one semester of study abroad. Upon completion of all requirements, students receive one degree and diploma from Universitas Indonesia.

  1. Double Degree (DD) / Joint Degree (JD)

A type of KKI Program that enables students to obtain two diplomas and/or two degrees upon completion of all academic requirements at both Universitas Indonesia and a partner university abroad.

  1. Fast Track Program (FTP)

A type of KKI Program that allows students to complete a Bachelor’s (S1) and Master’s (S2) degree in a shorter period of time than the regular pathway.

What are the admission requirements for international applicants?

For International Undergraduate Program (IUP/KKI), applicants are required to fulfil the following general requirements and documents.

General Requirements:

  1. High school or equivalent school graduates from the year 2022 onward.
  2. All documents are submitted online on the registration account.

Documents Requirements:

  1. Passport
  2. Latest color photograph (full face without hat, glasses, or accessories)
  3. High school or equivalent school certificate
  4. High school or equivalent school academic transcript
  5. Health certificate
  6. English proficiency must be demonstrated by one of the following certificates:
  • TOEFL (www.ets.org): TOEFL ITP 500 or TOEFL iBT 61;
  • IELTS (http://ielts.org) with minimum score 5.5;
  • FCE and CAE from Cambridge Main Suite Exams (https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/) with minimum score 154.

For the English proficiency score required, please refer to the guidelines for each faculty regulations.

Only TOEFL/IELTS/FCE/CAE certificates obtained from March 2024 onward are considered valid.

If you have or will receive a high school or equivalent school certificate from an institution where English is the primary language of instruction, you do not need to submit an English language proficiency certificate. Instead, you must provide a letter from your institution confirming that English is the primary language of instruction.

If you come from an English-speaking country and study in a school where English is the primary language of instruction, you should complete the letter of statement provided and submit it with the other documents when you do the enrolment.

How much is the living cost in Indonesia?

The estimated monthly living cost for students in Indonesia is approximately USD 678 per month.

Breakdown of expenses:

  • Off-Campus Housing: USD 200 – 264
  • Meals: USD 200
  • Books, Photocopies, and Supplies: USD 54
  • Transportation: USD 40
  • Communication & Internet (Wi-Fi): USD 20
  • Personal Monthly Needs (Water & Electricity): USD 67
  • Incidentals: USD 33

Total Estimated Monthly Cost: Approximately USD 678

What facilities are available on campus?

Universitas Indonesia provides extensive facilities that support students academically, socially, and personally, including a comprehensive central library, modern laboratories, research centers, student activity centers, sports and recreational facilities, on-campus accommodation, health services, and 24-hour security.

What is the admission selection process for Indonesian applicants?

The admission process for the KKI Program is conducted through SIMAK UI. Selection is based on a written test covering Basic Mathematics, English, Logic, and Quantitative Reasoning.

What are the steps in the application process?

The application process consists of the following steps:

  1. Create an account at admission.ui.ac.id Use your active email.
  2. Log in to your account. Choose the program you want to apply for.
  3. Complete the online form. Upload the latest photo and required documents.
  4. Pay the registration fee by transfer & upload the payment receipt to your account.
  5. Wait for the selection result
  6. Register as a student candidate if you are accepted
  7. Pay application fee by bank transfer to the following:
  • Bank Name: BNI
  • Branch: UI Depok
  • Account Name: Universitas Indonesia
  • Account Number: 127.300.0035
  • SWIFT Code: BNINIDJAUID

Application Fee: USD 150

Does SIMAK UI apply a passing grade?

SIMAK UI does not apply a passing grade. Admission is determined based on ranking, with applicants accepted according to the highest scores and the available quota set by Universitas Indonesia.
